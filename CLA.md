## 8. Signing

**Individual.** In the pull request that carries your first contribution,
comment exactly:

> I have read the KiChat Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and I agree to
> it on my own behalf.

Your GitHub account name and the date of that comment are the signature; the
maintainer records them in [CLA-signatures.md](CLA-signatures.md).

**Corporate.** If you contribute as part of your work, or your employer may
claim rights in what you write, ask the Maintainer (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) for the corporate form: the entity signs,
and lists the GitHub accounts it authorises to contribute under it. Those
accounts do not sign individually.

One agreement covers all your future contributions to the Project; you do not
sign again for each pull request.
